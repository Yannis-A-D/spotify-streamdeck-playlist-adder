Setup in 1 minute:
  1. Open Spotify Developer Dashboard and log in.
  2. Click Create app (Name: e.g. "Stream Deck").
  3. In Redirect URIs, add: http://127.0.0.1:4388/callback
  4. Copy your Client ID and Client Secret here and click Connect.
